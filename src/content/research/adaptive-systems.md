## The question

Many systems do not sit still while we study them. People change their behavior, relationships reorganize, and yesterday's intervention alters tomorrow's conditions. This research asks how those feedbacks shape collective outcomes.

## A visual overview

The basic loop is simple: **state influences choice, choice changes connections, and connections reshape state**. The interesting behavior comes from repeating that loop across a heterogeneous population.
