## Why tooling is part of the research

Software encodes decisions. A reproducible workflow makes those decisions inspectable—from raw data through transformation, model fitting, and the final figure.

## Design principles

1. Keep inputs immutable.
2. Make transformations explicit.
3. Attach figures to the code and data that produced them.
4. Prefer a readable workflow over a clever one.
